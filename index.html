Latest Book

The Age of Conquering the Seas

From Mare Liberum to Mare Dominium

Original Turkish edition: Denizlerin Fethi Çağı: Mare Liberum'dan Mare Dominium'a · Kaynak Yayınları · 2026 · ISBN 978-625-6953-91-8

What happens when technology makes it possible for states to exercise effective control over seas that international law still defines as free?

The book examines the emerging struggle over maritime spaces, strategic chokepoints and sea lanes, and develops Mare Dominium as a framework for understanding the changing relationship between technology, state power and the freedom of the seas.

Core Thesis

Mare Dominium

For centuries, the practical difficulty of controlling vast maritime spaces helped sustain the principle of the freedom of the seas. New surveillance, sensing, unmanned and long-range systems are changing the material conditions under which maritime power is exercised.

Mare Dominium explores the possibility that legal freedom at sea may increasingly coexist with forms of effective technological and strategic control. The concept is used to examine how shifts in technology and global power could reshape the future maritime order.

About

Tevfik Kadan

Tevfik Kadan is a Turkish journalist, author and researcher. His work focuses on maritime geopolitics, maritime strategy, sea power, maritime security, freedom of navigation and the changing international maritime order.

He is the author of Denizlerin Fethi Çağı: Mare Liberum'dan Mare Dominium'a (2026), a study of the relationship between technological change, state power, maritime law and effective control of the seas.
