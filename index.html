The Book

From maritime freedom to effective control

A historical, legal, technological and geopolitical inquiry into whether the physical conditions that sustained freedom of the seas are beginning to change.

Denizlerin Fethi Çağı

Mare Liberum'dan Mare Dominium'a

Tevfik Kadan · Kaynak Yayınları · 2026

The book traces the long tension between Mare Liberum and Mare Clausum, and asks whether satellites, persistent surveillance, unmanned systems, precision weapons and strategic chokepoints are altering the practical meaning of maritime freedom.

It brings together sea power, maritime law, global trade, energy security, emerging technology and the transition toward a multipolar international order.

Core Concept

Mare
Dominium

Formal legal freedom may increasingly coexist with effective technological and strategic control at sea.

For centuries, the difficulty of monitoring and controlling vast maritime spaces helped sustain the material conditions behind the freedom of the seas. Persistent sensors, satellites, unmanned systems, long-range precision strike and control of strategic chokepoints are changing that reality.

Mare Dominium describes an emerging condition in which a state need not formally own maritime space to acquire the capacity to monitor access, raise the cost of unwanted presence and exercise effective control.

Biography

About Tevfik Kadan

Tevfik Kadan is a Turkish journalist, author and researcher specializing in maritime geopolitics and strategic affairs.

He is Editor-in-Chief of Aydınlık newspaper. His work focuses on sea power, maritime security, the law of the sea, freedom of navigation, strategic chokepoints and the technological transformation of the international maritime order.

His 2026 book Denizlerin Fethi Çağı: Mare Liberum'dan Mare Dominium'a develops a multidisciplinary argument around the changing relationship between maritime freedom and effective control.
