Effective Maritime Control and the Transformation of the Freedom of the Seas

TEVFIK KADAN · 12 SEPTEMBER 2026 · ENGLISH · ZENODO · DOI 10.5281/ZENODO.22726599

A framework for analysing how technological change and strategic geography may transform the practical meaning of maritime freedom without necessarily changing its formal legal status.

The argument

For centuries, the difficulty of monitoring and controlling vast maritime spaces helped sustain the material conditions behind the freedom of the seas.

Persistent surveillance, networked sensors, unmanned systems, long-range precision weapons and control of strategic chokepoints are changing those conditions. The paper examines the growing gap that may emerge between formal legal freedom and effective maritime control.

Concept
MARE
DOMINIUM

Not necessarily formal ownership of maritime space, but the capacity to establish effective control through the combined effects of armed power, persistent technology and strategic geography.
