Mare Dominium: Effective Maritime Control and the Transformation of the Freedom of the Seas

Tevfik Kadan · Published 12 September 2026 · English · Zenodo

This paper develops the concept of Mare Dominium as a framework for analysing how technological change and strategic geography may transform the practical meaning of maritime freedom.

For centuries, the difficulty of monitoring and controlling vast maritime spaces helped sustain the material conditions behind the freedom of the seas. Persistent surveillance, networked sensors, unmanned systems, long-range precision weapons and control of strategic chokepoints are changing those conditions. The paper examines the growing gap that may emerge between formal legal freedom and effective maritime control.
