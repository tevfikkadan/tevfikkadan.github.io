Mare Dominium: Effective Maritime Control and the Transformation of the Freedom of the Seas

Tevfik Kadan · DOI 10.5281/zenodo.22726599

This paper develops the Mare Dominium framework to examine how persistent surveillance, precision strike, unmanned systems, networked sensors and strategic geography may enable forms of effective maritime control even where the formal legal principle of freedom of the seas remains unchanged.
